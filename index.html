Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can security services be deployed?

Emergency security services can be deployed within 2-4 hours for urgent situations. For standard deployments, we typically require 24-48 hours to ensure proper staffing and equipment setup. Guard dog services may require additional time for proper assessment and handler assignment.

Are your security personnel licensed and trained?

Yes, all our security personnel are fully licensed, bonded, and insured. They undergo comprehensive background checks and receive ongoing training in security protocols, emergency response, and customer service. Our guard dog handlers are specially certified in canine handling and safety procedures.

What types of properties do you secure?

We secure a wide range of properties including residential homes, commercial buildings, construction sites, warehouses, event venues, and industrial facilities. Our services are customized to meet the specific security needs of each property type and client requirements.

How does the guard dog service work?

Our guard dog service includes professionally trained dogs (German Shepherds, Rottweilers, Belgian Malinois) with certified handlers. The service includes initial security assessment, regular patrols, and 24/7 monitoring. Dogs are trained for deterrence and protection, and all handlers are equipped with communication devices for immediate response coordination.

What is included in your surveillance system installation?

Our surveillance package includes professional installation of HD cameras, DVR/NVR systems, remote monitoring setup, mobile app access, and basic training. We also provide ongoing technical support and maintenance. Additional features like motion detection, night vision, and cloud storage are available as add-ons.

Can I cancel or modify my service subscription?

Yes, you can modify or cancel your subscription with 30 days' notice. Some services may have specific terms outlined in your service agreement. We offer flexible plans that can be adjusted based on your changing security needs. Contact our customer service team for assistance with modifications or cancellations.

Still have questions? Contact our support team.
